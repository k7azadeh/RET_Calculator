How this estimate works

Transparent assumptions; no hidden calculations.

• Journey count = commute days/week × journeys/day × weeks/month + extra journeys/month.
• RET 20% and 40% Korting discounts are modelled as applying to the full fare you entered, because RET's 2026 MaandKorting terms state that the discount applies to both the base fare and kilometre fare.
• The default subscription prices are €7.90 and €28.70 as shown on RET's current product page. The OV-chipkaart-specific product terms PDF has also shown different prices, so confirm the price shown at purchase before buying.
• Regional pass prices are reference inputs only; coverage and product eligibility need to be checked separately.
